# Run: What year was the Eiffel Tower completed and who built it?



## Final Answer

It was designed and built by the company of civil engineer Gustave Eiffel for the 1889 World's Fair, held to celebrate the centennial of the French Revolution [S1]. The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower located on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France [S1]. His company, Eiffel et Cie, specialised in metal structural work and was responsible for the design and construction of the Eiffel Tower in Paris, completed in 1889 [S2]. Gustave Eiffel (1832-1923) was a French civil engineer [S2].
