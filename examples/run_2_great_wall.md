# Run: How long is the Great Wall of China and when was it built?



## Final Answer

The Great Wall of China is a series of fortifications built across the historical northern borders of China to protect against nomadic invasions [S1]. Construction of the various sections began as early as the 7th century BC, with the most famous sections built during the Ming Dynasty (1368-1644) [S1]. The Great Wall of China was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1987 [S2]. UNESCO describes it as one of the most impressive architectural achievements in human history, built and rebuilt over roughly two thousand years, from the 7th century BC through the Ming Dynasty [S2].
