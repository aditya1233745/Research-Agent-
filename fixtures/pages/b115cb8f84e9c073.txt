The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower located on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France. It was designed and built by the company of civil engineer Gustave Eiffel for the 1889 World's Fair, held to celebrate the centennial of the French Revolution. Construction began in 1887 and was completed on 31 March 1889. At the time of its completion, it was the tallest man-made structure in the world, a title it held for 41 years until the Chrysler Building in New York was finished in 1930. The tower stands 330 metres tall including antennas.
